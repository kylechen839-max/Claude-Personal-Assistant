Kwil is a small, invite-only beta run by one person, not a company. This page says what it stores, who else sees your data, and how to have it deleted. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking scripts, and your data is never sold.

Who sees what

Speech is turned into text on the Kwil server itself, and replies are spoken by a voice on the same server. Your recordings are not sent to any other company and are not kept after they are transcribed.

What is stored

  • Your account: the name you were invited under, your language, time zone and invite status. The invite link itself is kept only as a one-way hash.
  • Your conversations: their text and titles, in a file of your own on the server, so you can find and continue them. You can delete any conversation in the History screen; that overwrites its text.
  • Your Anthropic API key: your key is stored encrypted on the server and used only for your requests. We also keep a monthly estimate of the tokens and cost your key has used, shown only to you.
  • Your Google sign-in: a token for your own Calendar, used only for you. Disconnecting deletes it; you can also revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your Notion sign-in: a token for the Notion pages you shared, your workspace's name and the name of the Assignments database it found, stored encrypted and used only for you. Not your Notion email address. Disconnecting asks Notion to end access and deletes it.
  • Signed-in browsers: a session id and when it was last used, so you stay signed in. "Sign out everywhere" ends them.
  • Waitlist: the email address you gave, your optional note about what you'd use it for, and the date. It is used only to send you an invite by hand.
  • Logs: technical records such as times, lengths, counts and error types, and network (IP) addresses for rate limits and failed sign-ins. Not what you said, the replies, your email address or your notes.

The person who runs the server can technically read what is stored on its disk. Your data is not combined with anyone else's account, is not used for long-term memory, and is not used to train models by this service. Anthropic's handling of API data is set out in its own policies for your API account.

Deleting your data

In Settings you can download your data (your conversations and profile) and delete your account yourself. Deleting it asks Google and Notion to end Hey Claude's access. You can also ask at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} to have your account or your waitlist entry deleted. Removing an account signs you out everywhere and deletes your folder on the server: conversations, Google and Notion sign-ins and API key. The hosting company makes automatic backup snapshots of the disk, so a copy can remain in those snapshots until they expire. The person who runs the server can also download a backup of it; a copy in such a backup remains until they delete that file.

To stop Google access at any time, revoke Kwil at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To stop Notion access, remove Kwil in Notion under Settings, Connections. To stop API use, choose Remove key in Settings, or delete or rotate your key in the Anthropic Console.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. While you connect Google or Notion, a second, short-lived cookie ties that sign-in to your browser. Neither is shared with other sites, and there are no advertising or analytics cookies.
