Ask about your day. Out loud, or by typing.

Hey Claude is a voice and text assistant built on Claude. It reads your calendar and your Notion assignments, answers out loud, and asks you before it deletes anything or invites anyone.

Invite-only beta

Join the waitlist

What it does

  • Talk or type. Hold the mic, or use hands-free voice mode on your phone, and ask "what's on my calendar tomorrow?"
  • Your Google Calendar. Hear today's and tomorrow's events, add new ones and move them.
  • Your Notion assignments. List what's due and mark an assignment in progress or done.
  • Asks first. A delete, or an invite that emails other people, always needs your yes. While a web page or someone else's invite is part of the conversation, every change to your calendar or Notion asks first too.
  • Looks things up. It can search the web when a question needs it.
  • On your phone. It runs in the browser and can be added to your home screen like an app. No app store needed.
